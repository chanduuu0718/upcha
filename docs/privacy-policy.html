Upcha Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Upcha is a personal content workflow application used to prepare product-based promotional creatives and publish approved content to Pinterest.

Information we process

Upcha may process product URLs, product metadata, generated creative assets, Pinterest board information, and Pinterest authorization data required to operate the connected account.

Pinterest data

When you connect Pinterest, Upcha uses Pinterest OAuth to request only the permissions needed for creating and managing Pins and reading the connected user's boards. Pinterest credentials are never collected by Upcha. OAuth tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Affiliate links

Product URLs may be converted to affiliate destination links through the configured affiliate-link provider. The resulting destination URL is used when publishing an approved Pin.

Data sharing

Upcha does not sell personal information. Data is shared with connected services only as necessary to perform the workflow requested by the user.

Security

Application secrets and OAuth credentials should be stored in secure server-side configuration and not in source code.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding Upcha, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the Upcha application.
